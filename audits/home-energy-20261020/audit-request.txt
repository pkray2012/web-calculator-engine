### Specific mandatory checks

**Solar Financing**
- Verify nominal annual interest versus APR.
- Verify monthly payment, total interest, total payment, purchase rebate, later rebate, down payment, fees, and principal constraints.
- Check zero-interest floating-point behavior and ensure interest is not accidentally negative due to precision.
- Compare against the existing loan engine without modifying it.

**Solar Payback**
- Verify simple payback with and without user-entered annual maintenance.
- Check zero and negative net annual savings.
- Check that undefined payback is not described as “never.”
- Confirm that adjusted payback remains out of scope.
- Check all counterexamples and fractional-year interpolation assumptions.

**Charging calculations**
- Distinguish wall-side/source power from battery-side power.
- Apply efficiency exactly once.
- Confirm the meaning of usable battery capacity and state-of-charge percentages.
- Verify equal start and target SOC returns zero; target below start is invalid.
- Test charging-power caps and document that constant-power calculations do not model taper or auxiliary loads.
- Verify the EPA kWh/100-mile metric is not adjusted for charging losses a second time.

**Electric Bill and Utility Cost**
- Check units, annualization assumptions, fixed-charge treatment, zero totals, and the labels used for effective or residual rates.
- Do not claim the entered charges represent a universal tariff structure.
